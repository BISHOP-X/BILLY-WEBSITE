What can I do with Billy?+

Pay supported bills, buy or sell gift cards, access crypto services, purchase a prepaid virtual card, order foreign numbers, and place social-boost orders.

Do I need identity verification?+

Verification is required for protected actions such as crypto transactions and selling gift cards. Billy will tell you clearly when verification is needed.

Is Billy available to download now?+

Billy is currently in private testing on Android and iPhone. The official store links on this page will become active when the public release is ready.

How do I follow a transaction or order?+

Your Billy activity shows payments, orders, deliveries, refunds, and receipts with clear status updates.

What happens when a service is pending?+

Billy keeps the request visible while its final status is confirmed. You can follow updates from your activity instead of starting the same request again.
